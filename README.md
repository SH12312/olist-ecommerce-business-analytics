# E-Commerce Growth & Operational Performance Analytics

**SQL | Power BI | Excel | VBA**

An end-to-end business analytics portfolio project using the Brazilian Olist E-Commerce Dataset (2016–2018), covering approximately 100,000 orders.

## Project Overview

Analyzed commercial performance, delivery reliability, and customer purchasing behavior to identify business improvement opportunities. Applied nationwide market screening and assumption-driven scenario modeling to develop actionable management recommendations.

## Key Findings

- **Commercial Growth:** Item Sales Value grew **141.13% YoY** (Jan–Aug 2018 vs. 2017). SP Health & Beauty led state–category combinations in absolute sales growth (+R$225.9K).
- **Delivery Reliability:** RJ recorded 1,495 late deliveries and approximately 659 excess late deliveries relative to the national benchmark, making it a priority for operational investigation.
- **Customer Growth:** Only 3.00% of observed unique buyers placed multiple delivered orders, motivating further investigation of repeat purchasing.

## Scenario Modeling

Built Conservative, Base, and Upside scenarios in Excel, with sensitivity analysis and VBA automation.

| Scenario | Illustrative Base-Case Outcome |
|---|---|
| Commercial Growth | +R$29,815 in additional item sales |
| Delivery Improvement | 383 late deliveries avoided |
| Customer Growth | +R$34,260 in additional item sales |

*All scenario outcomes are hypothetical, not realized results. Item Sales Value excludes freight and is not a measure of profit.*

## Tools & Methodology

- **SQL (SQLite):** Data preparation, validation, analytical views, nationwide market screening, and business analysis.
- **Power BI:** Two-page interactive dashboard analyzing commercial and operational performance.
- **Excel:** Scenario modeling, assumption management, and sensitivity analysis.
- **VBA:** Model reset and scenario-summary PDF export automation.

## Data Source

Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist, available on [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce).

**Analytical limitations:** Historical observations do not establish causation. The repeat-buyer share is not a formal retention rate. Scenario outcomes exclude intervention costs and should not be interpreted as financial forecasts.
